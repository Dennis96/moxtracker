MOX is paused

The MOX website and online services are off for now. There is no restart date yet.

What changes for you

  • Features that use the internet don't respond: account, Meta and Draft stats from the website, sharing games and Drafts, Research and updates.
  • No new MOX versions are released and downloads are closed.
  • The data you chose to share stays stored as described in the privacy notice. It isn't used or shown to anyone.
  • To have your data deleted, or to get a copy of it, write to privacy@moxtracker.app.
